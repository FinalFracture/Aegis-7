# QA-03: Lighting, Materials & Visuals pass (Cylinder Hab-7)
Date: 2026-11-01 | Tester: QA-03 | Base commit: 39eba42 (main) | Godot 4.7
Method: static review of scenes/preface/preface_playable_area.tscn, scripts/preface_playable_area.gd, project.godot, plus a headless `--validate-only` boot (no GPU renders captured).
Headless boot log: `[CYL-WINDOW] removed_wedge=686 removed_caps=1024 removed_map=89 a0_deg=-17.000`, nav edge-merge warning (8 edges), RID/ObjectDB leaks at exit.

Summary: 11 bugs. High 3, Med 5, Low 3. No Critical.

## VIS-01 [High] Renderer is Compatibility, but the environment was tuned for Forward+
- Location: project.godot `[rendering] renderer/rendering_method="gl_compatibility"` (config/features still says "Forward Plus"); Environment_pref_play in preface_playable_area.tscn
- SSAO (ssao_enabled, intensity 1.4), SSR, and the soft shadow filter quality settings are ignored or limited on gl_compatibility. Contact shadows and AO never render, so props float and corners look flat.
- Repro: launch the main scene and compare corners/under-furniture with SSAO on and off. There is no difference. `RenderingServer.get_current_rendering_method()` returns gl_compatibility.

## VIS-02 [High] Sun light leaks through the opaque drum hull outside the 45 m shadow range
- Location: preface_playable_area.gd `_build_lighting` around L2280 (`directional_shadow_max_distance = 45.0`), and `_process` L3171-3175 (sun orbits with the starfield)
- The sun is a single DirectionalLight3D inside a closed 800 m x 400 m drum. Past 45 m there is no shadow cascade, so the hull casts no shadow, and the sun lights interior surfaces as if the hull were missing. Only the 36 degree window wedge should admit sunlight.
- Repro: stand on the promenade, look down-axis toward an end cap, and wait for the sun to orbit away from the window wedge. Distant interior surfaces stay sunlit.

## VIS-03 [High] The orbiting sun lights the deck from below or through floors at some starfield angles
- Location: preface_playable_area.gd L3175 `_sun.basis = Basis(Vector3.RIGHT, _starfield_cylinder.rotation.x) * _sun_base_basis` (ROT_SPEED = TAU/240)
- Over each 4-minute revolution, the sun direction sweeps a full 360 degrees around X. For about half the cycle the light comes from under the deck: L2 slabs are lit from below, shadows invert, and the "golden hour" atrium comment no longer applies. There is no clamp and no fade of energy when the sun is occluded.
- Repro: run the main scene, idle about 2 minutes, and watch the atrium floor or L1 ceiling lighting.

## VIS-04 [Med] Blanket self-emission on drum materials flattens lighting and ignores textures
- Location: preface_playable_area.gd about L1400-1414 (`dup.emission = bm.albedo_color * 0.22` for every opaque drum surface)
- Every opaque drum surface glows at 22% of albedo. This kills night-side contrast, makes the hull look self-lit, and is unaffected by emergency lighting. It uses albedo_color, not the albedo texture, so textured surfaces get a flat tint wash.
- Repro: enable emergency lighting (toggle_lighting). The drum interior stays visibly bright and evenly lit.

## VIS-05 [Med] Emergency lighting toggle doesn't restore the authored values, and it doesn't dim the luminaires
- Location: `_set_emergency_lighting` about L2366-2382
- On restore, fill goes to 0.34 (authored 0.28) and ambient goes to 0.36 (authored 0.42 in the .tscn), so the scene changes after one on/off cycle. When it is turned on, the omni task lights only change colour; their energy stays at 0.7-0.9, so "emergency" red is as bright as daylight. The SpotLights and the drum emission (VIS-04) are not touched.
- Repro: press toggle_lighting twice and compare a screenshot against a fresh boot.

## VIS-06 [Med] Observation window glass is effectively invisible, with an unrealistic material
- Location: `_cut_observation_window` about L1417-1424 (`Mat_ObservationWindowGlass`: alpha 0.06, metallic 0.85, roughness 0.06)
- At alpha 0.06 there is no readable pane. Metallic 0.85 on a dielectric is physically wrong, and with no sky reflections on Compatibility the glass shows almost nothing. The window reads as a hole in the hull: no frame mullions, no tint, no Fresnel.
- Repro: look up through the wedge from the deck. You cannot tell whether glass is present.

## VIS-07 [Med] Glass strip and both end-cap discs share one double-sided alpha mesh, which causes sorting errors
- Location: `_cut_observation_window` L1430-1490 (one ArrayMesh `ObservationWindowGlass`, CULL_DISABLED, TRANSPARENCY_ALPHA)
- Godot sorts transparent objects per instance origin, not per triangle. The 800 m strip and both 200 m-radius cap discs sort as one object against the starfield, prop glass, and the force-field doors (also alpha). Back and front faces render in arbitrary order, so expect popping or wrong layering when looking through a cap disc at the strip, or through a force-field door at the window.
- Repro: stand near an end cap and look across toward the opposite cap through the window strip and a force-field door. Pan slowly and watch for layering flicker.

## VIS-08 [Med] Sky reflections disagree with the black space background
- Location: preface_playable_area.tscn Environment (`reflected_light_source = 2` Sky, ProceduralSkyMaterial blue-grey) vs `_ready` L355-358 forcing `background_mode = BG_COLOR` black
- Metals and glass still reflect a blue-grey procedural sky while space is black, and the ReflectionProbe (UPDATE_ONCE, intensity 0.3) bakes at startup, before streamed interiors exist.
- Repro: look at a metallic rail (rail_mat) near the window. You get a blue-grey sheen with nothing to reflect.

## VIS-09 [Low] Starfield end caps are stretched and distorted
- Location: `_build_starfield_outer_cylinder` L4499-4535 (CylinderMesh cap_top/bottom true, uv1_scale 6x3, one texture)
- CylinderMesh cap UVs with a 6x3 scale smear the stars radially. Navigation beacons with diffraction crosses become streaks on the end-cap vista. It is also linear+mipmaps at 2200 m, so the stars shimmer and fade with distance.
- Repro: on the promenade, look along +X (the QA t38 vista pose, L6452) at the starfield end disc.

## VIS-10 [Low] Prop glass is forced to a single grey-blue tint, and every prop surface is double-sided
- Location: material import loop about L2440-2460 (`smat.cull_mode = CULL_DISABLED` for all; glass albedo forced to Color(0.35,0.42,0.47, 0.22-0.30))
- The authored glass/lens/holo colours are discarded. CULL_DISABLED on all opaque surfaces causes backface lighting artifacts (dark inner faces visible through gaps) and extra overdraw.
- Repro: inspect any tinted glass prop or holo lens. They all show the same grey-blue.

## VIS-11 [Low] The recreation grass slab is a flat, untextured colour
- Location: `_add_recreation_grass_slab` about L1562 (`grass_mat.albedo_color = Color(0.24,0.52,0.18)`, no texture, CULL_DISABLED)
- The 36 degree wedge opposite the window is solid uniform green with no texture or normal map, so it looks unfinished when seen through the window and across the drum.
- Repro: look across the drum to angle about 163 degrees (log `[CYL-REC] grass slab a_deg=163.000`).
