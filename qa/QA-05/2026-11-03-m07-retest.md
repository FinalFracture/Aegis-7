## New bugs
- **SYS-14 (low) — kerb ramp side faces snag the player.** `Ext_45_ext_kerb_ramp_953` (top y 0.295, x≈9.0–10.6) next to paving at y 0.171: walking E or W along z -14 / -15 stops dead at x=8.67 / x=10.83 (repro both ways, 2 z rows). That's a ~12 cm vertical side face the player cannot step onto from the side, though it can walk up the ramp lengthwise. Suggest flared or sloped ramp sides in the collider, or enough step-up to clear 0.13 m.
- **SYS-15 (low) — west road curb is one-way.** At z -5 the player drops off the curb strip (x≈-17) onto the road but cannot walk back up: stuck at (-17.39, 0.05, -5.00) holding W. Check that the road side has a kerb ramp or step-up path back; otherwise it's a soft trap. Probably the same step-height limit as SYS-14.
