## Root cause shared by SYS-14, SYS-15 and SYS-21 (code reading, report only)
Capsule radius 0.35, `floor_max_angle` 50°, step-up only runs when the hit normal has `dot(up) < 0.5`. On a square edge of height h the contact normal has `dot(up) = (0.35 − h)/0.35`. For h ≈ 0.125–0.175 m the normal is steeper than 50° (so `move_and_slide` treats it as a wall) but its `dot(up)` is ≥ 0.5 (so the step-up skips it). Edges in that band are a dead stop. The kerb ramp side face (~0.12–0.13 m) and the west curb (~0.12–0.17 m) sit in that band, as does the 0.15 m test ledge. Suggested fix: allow the step for `dot(up) < cos(floor_max_angle)` (≈ 0.643) instead of 0.5, or retry the step test when the slide ends with a wall hit and no progress.
