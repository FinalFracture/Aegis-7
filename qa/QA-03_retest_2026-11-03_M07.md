## New bugs

### VIS-20 (Medium): curb MultiMeshes are distance-culled as one group, so nearby curbs vanish
- Cause: `_ext_batch_multimesh` copies `visibility_range_end` 40 m and margin 4 m from the first member onto each MultiMeshInstance3D. Godot measures the visibility range for the whole MMI, so the cull depends on the distance to the group's AABB rather than to each curb. Groups: ExtMM_20/21 ext_curb_segment (159; AABB 93.9 x 46.7 m, center (0.0, 0.2, 1.4)), ExtMM_22/23 894mm (14), ExtMM_24/25/26 corner (36; center (-1.4, 0.2, 7.2)).
- Effect: curbs right next to the player disappear when the camera is far from the group's center, and the whole curb set pops at once. Distant curbs that the per-node 40 m cull used to skip are now drawn whenever the group is "in range".
- Repro: camera at (45, 1.7, -20) looking at (30, 0.2, -16). BEFORE shows the curbs and reflector strips along the tram lanes a few metres ahead. AFTER has them all missing (3.9% of pixels differ). Poses at (0,2,-40), (0,2,-43) and (30,2,-14) showed no visible loss.
- Suggested fix (not applied): set visibility_range_end to 0 on the curb MMIs, or split the curb groups into spatial cells, or leave curbs out of the 40 m cull.

No other new issues. The props and decals that are not MultiMeshed keep their per-node 40 m / 25 m ranges.
