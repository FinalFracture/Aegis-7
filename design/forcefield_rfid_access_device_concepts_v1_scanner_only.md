# Force-Field Door Wall RFID Access Device: 5 Concepts (Vance, Oct 28 2026)
Mount: flush pocket on +Z side of door frame, default 0.14 W x 0.24 H m, wall 0.30 m deep; nothing proud of the wall face by more than 5 mm.
Shared states: standby amber (#FF9500, restrained); granted green #3FAF6A; denied = two short amber blinks then back to standby.
Shared frame tie-in: stepped pearl (#D8DEE6) / graphite (#282E38) bezel matching the door frame; on grant, light hands off into the frame's conduit light pipe and runs up to the corner emitters, where the violet field drops. No floating text; markings are crisp textures only.

## 1. Tendon Harp (reads the neural-tendon sleeve)
- Core idea: it reads the rhythm of the wearer's neural-tendon sleeve, not a badge. Nine thin graphite reeds sit behind smoked alpha glass and act as a physical readout of the sleeve's pulse.
- [F] interaction: wrist within 0.10 m; reeds deflect one at a time in a wave that mirrors the sleeve's Battery_Vein trace pulse.
- Standby: reeds still, one amber hairline along the base. Scanning: the wave runs, and amber climbs the reed tips. Granted: reeds settle flush, tips turn green and feed the frame light pipe.
- Materials/silhouette: tall narrow pearl slab with a stepped graphite bezel; full-height vertical glass window with a comb of reeds behind it. Fits 0.14 x 0.24.

## 2. Spin Governor (tied to habitat spin)
- Core idea: force fields need to phase-lock to the drum's rotation, so the panel shows a small brushed-steel gyro disc turning in sync with Hab-7's spin. The door opens only once the local emitter is locked.
- [F] interaction: wrist over the lower graphite palm plate; the disc spins up and its index tick sweeps into a fixed notch in the bezel, ending in an audible detent click.
- Standby: disc turns slowly, carrying an amber index tick. Scanning: the disc spins up and the tick hunts for the notch. Granted: disc locks and the notch ring turns green.
- Story hook: in Chapter 1 the disc visibly wobbles at 0.35G and 0.12G (Mechanic C), so the same prop reads gravity state.
- Materials/silhouette: upper recessed round well (0.12 dia) with a glass lid over a brushed-steel disc, and a lower flat graphite plate. Pocket 0.14 x 0.26.

## 3. Pressure Lung (life-support check)
- Core idea: a real habitat checks pressure and O2 on both sides before letting a person through a field barrier. A small pearl membrane breathes with the life-support cycle beside twin glass sight columns, one per side of the door.
- [F] interaction: the membrane draws in and holds while the two fluid columns move toward equal.
- Standby: membrane breathes slowly; amber meniscus in both columns. Scanning: the membrane holds and the columns move. Granted: columns level, and a green band lights at the equal line. Hazard: columns stay mismatched and amber stays on, even with a valid wrist.
- Materials/silhouette: graphite face with a domed pearl elastomer membrane (top) and two slim vertical alpha-glass tubes (bottom) etched with tick marks. Pocket 0.14 x 0.30.

## 4. Petal Iris Socket (kinetic reveal)
- Core idea: a closed flush hex of six stepped pearl petals irises open to reveal a recessed induction cup only when a sleeve approaches. It keeps dust and debris out and reads clearly as mechanical.
- [F] interaction: petals rotate open (camera iris, 0.09 m aperture), and the cup slides forward 8 mm to stay flush, then takes the reading.
- Standby: closed hex with amber light in the petal seams. Scanning: iris open and the cup's ring of light pipes chases amber. Granted: ring turns green, and the petals half-close, leaving a green hex rim lit while the field is down.
- Materials/silhouette: graphite plate, pearl petals with graphite step edges, and a brushed-steel cup. Fits 0.14 x 0.24.

## 5. Lenticular Emblem Stack (physical "hologram")
- Core idea: a stack of ~12 thin etched-glass slats on individual pivots. Only when every slat lines up does the etched district emblem (D-01 to D-04) resolve, giving a hologram effect with no projection. It also tells the player which department zone they are entering.
- [F] interaction: slats rotate into alignment one after another, bottom to top, with a soft ratchet.
- Standby: slats offset so the emblem is scrambled into shards; amber edge-lit. Scanning: slats click into line, and amber follows the aligned ones. Granted: the emblem resolves crisp, and the edge light turns green.
- Materials/silhouette: deep graphite well with a pearl stepped bezel and a vertical stack of alpha-glass slats edge-lit from the sides; emblem etched as texture. Pocket 0.14 x 0.24, slats occupy 0.12 m of the wall depth.
