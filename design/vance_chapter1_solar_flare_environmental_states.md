## 1. Preface Architectural Gating & Wordless Environmental Teaching (*The Super Metroid Standard*)

### 1.1 Core Design Mandate: Zero Text, Zero Voiceover, 100% Architectural Affordance (`5 Min Experienced / 30 Min First-Time`)
Per Jared's directive (`[t37u]`):
- **Absolute Ban on Traditional Tutorials:** There are **zero on-screen button prompts, zero UI tutorial pop-ups, zero AI voiceovers, and zero crewmates explaining controls or mechanics**. We assume the player is a competent orbital maintenance technician who understands their job and learns by reading physical space, light, sound, and motion at a steady, escalating pace—completable in **5 minutes by an experienced player** who immediately recognizes the physical grammar, or **30 minutes by a thorough first-time player** experimenting with each room's tactile geometry.
- **How Architecture Replaces Text (*Super Metroid* Dachora / Etecoon Method):**
  1. **Sightlines & `alphaMode: "BLEND"` Glass Demonstrations Before Interaction:** Every mechanic (`Mechanic B` dog-latches/patch-bays, `Mechanic C` centrifugal RPM gravity shifts, `Mechanic A` cross-cylinder Coriolis chord launches) is **first witnessed visually through a translucent `alphaMode: "BLEND"` window or open atrium**—often performed autonomously by an **Aegis-7 Service Android (`Unit A7-M4`)** or automated industrial machinery on the other side of a glass partition—before the player is ever locked in a room that requires that skill to exit.
  2. **Self-Correcting Physical Lock-In Gates ("Noob Bridges" & Single-Verb Thresholds):** Each Preface room (`Room 01` -> `Room 02` -> `Room 10` -> `bldg_16` Drydock -> `Valley A` Promenade) isolates **one physical maintenance or traversal verb** behind a natural architectural choke point whose solution is visibly modeled in the room's geometry and lighting (`#D95B26` signal-orange mechanical touchpoints, `#FF9500` amber unpowered conduits, `#00E5FF` cyan energized circuits).

## 2. Architectural Vision: One Living Megastructure Across Three Dramatic States
Rather than building disposable one-off level geometry, **every single one of our 20 locked Aegis-7 Building Exteriors (`bldg_01`–`bldg_20`), 10 Interior Rooms (`Room 01`–`Room 10`), 5 Interior Macro-Setpieces (`interior_setpiece_01`–`05`), and 5 Street Props (`prop_01`–`05`)** is engineered as a **3-State Transformable Movie Set** (*2001: A Space Odyssey*, *Alien*, *Elysium*, *Interstellar*, *The Martian*):

1. **State 1 — THE PREFACE (`5 Min Experienced / 30 Min New Player` | "Nominal Golden-Hour Aldrin Cycler & Wordless *Super Metroid* Gating"):**
   - A thriving, bustling `1.00G` (`1.98–2.09 RPM`) Earth–Mars orbital transit metropolis during a routine hyperbolic planet flyby pickup/drop-off window. Through pure architectural gating, sightlines through `alphaMode: "BLEND"` glass, and autonomous Android/capsule demonstrations (`Stages P1–P5` above), the player masters `Mechanic B` (tactile maintenance & Android co-op), `Mechanic C` (centrifugal RPM gravity shifts), and `Mechanic A` (cross-cylinder Coriolis chord ballistics) without a single word of UI text or voiceover.
2. **State 2 — CHAPTER 1 CLIMAX (`0:30 – 2:35` | "Perihelion Solar Flare Impact & Chain-Reaction Cascade"):**
   - A massive Coronal Mass Ejection (CME) slams into *Cylinder Hab-7* at perihelion. The architecture undergoes a visceral three-stage physical cascade:
     - **Stage 2A (CME Flash & EMP/Thermal Surge):** Blinding `12,000K` white-violet/amber coronal over-bloom on the Axial Sun-Core (`interior_setpiece_03`), seized heliostat louvers, and EMP-scrambled Android/AI kiosks (`prop_02`).
     - **Stage 2B (Spin-Drive Quench & Low-G Hydro Detachment — `Mechanic C`):** D-He3 magnetic pinch quench in `bldg_12` / `Room 09` drops drum spin from `1.00G` -> `0.35G` -> `0.12G`; `Valley B`'s Meandering Ring-River and Stepped Waterfall Dam (`interior_setpiece_02`, `bldg_09`) detach from their basins into floating `alphaMode: "BLEND"` cyan/emerald water ribbons and globules arching across the 400m sky.
     - **Stage 2C (Cross-Cylinder Chord Line-of-Sight Triage — `Mechanic A`):** With circumferential ring-corridors (`Room 02`) and the `120°` Suspension Skybridge (`interior_setpiece_01`) locked down by pressure bulkheads, the technician uses **direct 400m ceiling-to-floor visual sightlines** and cross-chord laser/payload launches between `Valley A` (`180°`), `Valley B` (`60°`), and `Valley C` (`300°`) to sequence the multi-building recovery.
3. **State 3 — END OF CHAPTER 1 (`2:35 – 3:00` | "Stabilized But Scarred — Solar Escape Trajectory & Incoming Earth Rescue Fleet"):**
   - Life support (`bldg_06`, `bldg_07`, `bldg_04`) and auxiliary spin-gravity (`0.85G`) are restored—the 50,000 passengers and crew are safe from immediate asphyxiation or radiation—but the architecture wears permanent **thermal carbonization scars, dimmed auxiliary amber/cyan sun-core baffles, and cordoned `#D95B26` blast bulkheads**. In the final wordless visual reveal inside `Room 07` (Command Bridge) and `bldg_11` / `bldg_08`, the 3D holographic orbital table shifts from the green/cyan Earth–Mars Aldrin Cycler loop to an **amber/crimson hyperbolic escape trajectory slipping out of the Sun's gravity well**, while `bldg_08`'s deep-space dishes slew and lock onto the **incoming Earth Rescue Fleet** racing to intercept Hab-7.
